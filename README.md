This is the detail almost no coupon article spells out, and it changes how you should sequence your orders.

Meanwhile, Kohl's Cash also can't be used on Sephora at Kohl's products, gift cards, shipping, or to pay your Kohl's Card bill, and it expires on the exact redemption dates printed on the coupon — no grace period before or after.

So if you earn $10 Kohl's Cash on one order and plan to redeem it later, don't redeem it on the order you're running through TopCashback. The clean approach: use Kohl's Cash on a separate, standalone transaction where you weren't counting on cashback anyway, and keep your coupon-plus-cashback order free of it.

Save it, print it, or add it to the Kohl's Wallet in the app, where you can also set an expiration reminder.

## The order everything applies in (and why it changes your math)

Kohl's applies dollar-off amounts before sitewide percentage discounts. That ordering rule is confirmed on Kohl's own help pages, and it works in your favor.

Take a $60 kids' clothing order with KIDS10 and TAKE20:

1. KIDS10 applies first: $60 − $10 = $50
2. TAKE20 applies to the reduced total: $50 − 20% = **$40**

If the percentage applied first, you'd land at $42. Same codes, different sequence, $2 difference — and the gap widens on bigger orders. Note also that Kohl's Cash earning is calculated on the post-discount, pre-tax amount, so stacking the codes first also maximizes the Kohl's Cash you earn toward your next standalone order.

- Sephora at Kohl's — no percent codes, no Kohl's Cash earning or redemption

- Kohl's Cares charitable items
- Emerging brands and Kohl's online-exclusive items

Shipping has its own math: free standard shipping at $49, otherwise $8.95, with delivery in 3–6 days. Free in-store pickup skips the fee without forfeiting your TopCashback cashback, which makes it the obvious choice for orders under $49.
